Operator controls & calibration

Floor calibration

Fix the camera in place. Mark a rectangle on the floor. Enter its measured dimensions, then click: near-left → near-right → far-right → far-left. Map origin is the near-edge centre, not the camera. Bounding-box feet are approximate; occluded feet can be inaccurate.

No floor calibration. Map positions are illustrative.

Presentation controls

Signal-loss testing only interrupts this browser's simulation. Camera and inference continue. Each display has its own selected target and simulation.

Depth estimate: unavailable

Depth map (button under the camera): red is near, blue is far (scale 0–6 m), black has no depth. White box: the region measured on the selected person, drawn only on the frame it was measured on. Stereo distance and the map position it gives are unvalidated; without stereo distance the simulated vehicle brakes and holds.

A one-lens depth estimate is never used to position the vehicle on this page. The virtual gimbal is a crop, not a rendered airborne viewpoint.
